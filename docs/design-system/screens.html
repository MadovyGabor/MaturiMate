MaturiMate sample screens

Three screens in the design system colors, light and dark theme side by side. The app text is Hungarian; the name, date and progress are made-up examples.

Onboarding: daily goal

One question per screen. Step counter at the top, choices as chips, one main button at the bottom.

Practice: after checking

The wrong choice gets a red X, the right one a green check, and the explanation slides up from the bottom. Color is never the only signal.

Home

Countdown, the gold daily-goal bar, the streak and the next lesson. Navigation bar at the bottom.

Contrast

WCAG contrast ratios of the main color pairs. Text needs at least 4.5, borders and icons at least 3. Every pair passes with one exception: in the light theme the gold progress fill has only 1.4 against its empty track, so a gold bar always shows its value as text too ("7 / 10 perc").
