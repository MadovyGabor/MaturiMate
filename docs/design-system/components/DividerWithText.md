# DividerWithText

A thin line with a short text in the middle, to separate two ways of doing the same thing ("vagy e-maillel" between the Google button and the e-mail fields).

- **The caller provides:** the text.
- Lines in `outline-variant`, the text in `label-medium`, `on-surface-variant`, `space-3` (12 dp) around the text.
- Compose: `AppDividerWithText` in `core/presentation/uiComponents/AppDividerWithText.kt`; used by the account form and the sign-in screen.
